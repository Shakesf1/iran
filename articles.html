How the US plans to end the Iran War?

Trump has been vocal about having won the war and that Iran is desperate for a deal. The US supposedly proposed a 15 point plan to Iran. Elements of the plan include:

  • Nuclear disarmament, dismantle nuclear program and hand over all enriched uranium
  • Limitations on missiles etc.
  • Sanctions would be lifted and assistance provided for civil nuclear energy
  • Iran to guarantee unconditional safe passage for all international shipping

Trump also stated Iran is willing to hand a "very big gift" related to oil and gas. A peace team has been assembled from the US side with JD Vance, Marco Rubio, Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. Intended location is Islamabad in Pakistan.

In essence what the US offers is to restore global energy stability, no real regime change and Iran has to give up any and all nuclear arms. Now and in the future.

At first glance it doesn't look like a bad deal to Iran. However, they were still in talks about the nuclear program when they got attacked. They also had a deal with the US before (Obama) limiting Uranium enrichment which Trump threw out of the window. No actions of the US will have instilled any form of confidence that a deal is actually a deal. Giving up any sort of control over Hormuz would remove Iran's strongest weapon. Missiles would remove their capability for asymmetric warfare.

That is the problem of Trump incompetence. He lost all credibility. What has Iran said regarding the peace talks? A spokesperson for the Iran Military, Zolfaghari, gave a pre-recorded televised address where he mocked the US efforts to pull out of the war it started alongside Israel.

"Don't call your failure an agreement"
"Have your internal conflicts reached the point where you are negotiating with yourself?"
"Our first and last word has been the same from day one, and it will stay that way: Someone like us will never come to terms with someone like you. Not now, not ever."

> Zolfaghari, spokesperson for the Iran Military

This followed Trump's TACO of a day earlier regarding the peace talks which was denied by many actors in Iran.

It is still unknown who the US has been 'negotiating' with. However, it doesn't completely matter. It's likely not with senior leaders in Iran as they are threatened with their lives after all the executions by US/Israel. Knowing the history, e.g. US throwing out the 'Obama' treaty and recently striking when negotiating, it's not likely they will give up anything of power (Hormuz, missiles, threat of Nuclear) as that just weakens them for the next round.

The most probable US play is to pull a rabbit out of a hat. Find an opportunistic Iranian, a second or third-rate figure in the current regime, who sees this as a personal path to power. Push him forward, have him sign a deal, and let Trump claim victory. The logic relies on a large enough slice of the Iranian population being so desperate to rid themselves of the current regime that they accept the lesser of two evils. Ignore the existing chain of command, build your own, and hope the people fall in line. In essence: divide, conquer, and claim the oil. Not too dissimilar from Venezuela.

The flaw is structural. The IRGC is not a bureaucracy that collapses when its leadership is decapitated. It is an ideology with guns, money, and deep roots in Iranian society. The US is betting that executing enough senior figures has loosened that grip. That is an enormous wager on a theory that has failed in almost every comparable context.

Conclusion

The US will likely manufacture a deal. On paper. A compliant figure, a signing ceremony, a victory lap. But a deal signed with someone who does not control the IRGC is not a deal that controls Iran.

What happens on the ground may bear very little resemblance to what might be announced in Islamabad.
