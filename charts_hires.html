Strategic Escalation Score

A multi-factor composite index measuring theater intensity. Values > 1 signal active escalation; values < −1 indicate significant cooling. Integrates four pillars: Kinetics (launch acceleration), Maritime Friction (VLCC crossings), Energy Risk (Murban–Brent spread) and Economic Cost (log-change BDTI). Updates intraday every 30 min.

Hormuz Gate: VLCC / VLGC Transits (Crude Oil)

Daily VLCC transit estimates derived from AIS position snapshots. A vessel is counted as a chokepoint transit when sequential positions are observed on opposite sides of the Strait of Hormuz geofence. Green bars = eastbound (loaded crude outbound); red bars = westbound (return). Darker shades indicate sanctioned vessels.
