War Escalation Monitor: Iran

Monitoring the cost-asymmetry of regional defense and escalation.

Regional defenses mostly hold against Iranian drone and missile swarms. However, the strategic risk lies in economic attrition & resource depletion. This page monitors the amount of missiles and drones launched by Iran, how many are intercepted, and how many get through to their targets.

The goal is to track the evolving cost asymmetry of this conflict in near real-time. We also monitor casualties and track ships through the Strait of Hormuz.

Strategic Escalation Score

A combined index of launch acceleration and geographic spread. Scores > 2.0 signal a major shift in operational tempo. Incident data are aggregated hourly to measure attack tempo, with a 6-hour rolling mean capturing recent activity and a 24-hour rolling mean representing the baseline, producing a tempo change ratio that signals acceleration. The number of distinct locations affected each hour is used to capture geographic spread. These components are combined into a weighted Escalation Score (70% tempo, 30% spread) to provide a time-series indicator of operational intensity and spatial expansion, stored for monitoring and visualization.

Hormuz: Oil spread

Graph below shows the price of Oman Crude Oil as well as the spread versus Murban. Murban is higher quality oil but not dependent on shipping through Hormuz while Oman Crude is. Therefore changes in the spread are indicative of the impact of the Hormuz closure. Higher spread = more impact (normal spread is slightly negative).

Maritime Economic Risk (BDTI)

Tracking the daily cost of crude oil shipping via the Baltic Dirty Tanker Index. This primarily tracks Very Large Crude Carriers (VLCCs) which carry heavy crude oil. Therefore good proxy. Also reflects hidden costs such as insurance, fuel and security premia.

Hormuz Gate: VLCC/VLGC Transits (Crude Oil)

Vessel transit estimates are derived from AIS position snapshots. A vessel is counted as a chokepoint transit when sequential positions are observed on opposite sides of a defined geofence surrounding the Strait of Hormuz. Erroneous data is filtered out based on geo data.
