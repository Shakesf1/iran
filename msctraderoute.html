Monitoring the Sea-Land-Sea bypass of the Strait of Hormuz

The MSC Land Bridge serves as the primary multimodal alternative to the blocked Strait of Hormuz. By offloading at Red Sea ports and utilizing Saudi Arabia's "Land Bridge" trucking and rail corridor to Dammam, carriers attempt to maintain Gulf supply chains. We track the Inflow (Mother vessels at Jeddah/KAP) against the Outflow (Feeders at Dammam) to measure the efficiency of this bypass.
